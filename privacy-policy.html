Effective date: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Zek Tycoon (the “Game”), developed by [Developer name] (“we”, “us”), how it is used and what choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or address, and we do not collect them inside the Game. The Game and the services it uses may collect the following:

Game progress (money, workshops, upgrades, purchases you made in the Game)To save your progress on the device and in the cloud, so you can continue after reinstalling or on another device.

2. Third-party services

The Game uses the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Analytics, Authentication, Cloud Save, In-App Purchasing) and Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) — Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Advertising networks used through LevelPlay to show rewarded ads (for example Unity Ads, Google AdMob and others). Each network processes data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing — Google Privacy Policy

3. Ads

The Game only shows ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a reward (rewarded ads). Ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Your consent and choices

  • When you first start the Game, we ask you to accept this Privacy Policy. Analytics and ads start only after that.
  • You can play without connecting Google Play; your progress is then saved on the device and linked to a random player ID.
  • You can delete your local progress at any time: Settings → Reset.

5. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA or UK under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing. To request deletion of your cloud save and analytics data, contact us at [contact e-mail] and tell us the player ID shown in the Game, or the Google account you connected.

6. Data retention

Cloud saves are kept while you play and are deleted on request. Analytics data is kept in aggregated or pseudonymous form for as long as needed to improve the Game, in line with the retention periods of the services listed above.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we make important changes, the Game will ask you to accept the new version.
