Zek Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play Zek Tycoon (the “Game”), developed by [Developer name] (“we”, “us”, “our”), why it is processed, who it is shared with and what choices you have.

In short:

  • We never ask for your real name, e-mail, phone number, address or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device and backed up to the cloud under a random player ID.
  • We use anonymous gameplay analytics and optional rewarded ads only after you accept this policy.
  • The leaderboard shows a nickname you choose (or a random one) and your income per second.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Game and save your progress on the device and in the cloud, so you can continue after reinstalling the Game or on another device.
  • To show the leaderboard and your place on it.
  • To improve the Game: we look at gameplay analytics to find where players get stuck and how to balance the Game. We do not use analytics to identify you personally.
  • To show rewarded ads you choose to watch, give you the reward and prevent ad fraud.
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases and to prevent the same purchase from being granted twice.
  • To protect the Game against cheating: the Game checks the current time with a time server, so timers (offline earnings, daily rewards, boosts) cannot be manipulated by changing the device clock.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on your consent (analytics and advertising), on the performance of our agreement with you (saving your progress, delivering purchases, the leaderboard) and on our legitimate interests (security, fraud and cheat prevention).

4. Rewarded ads

The Game shows only rewarded ads: ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, for example doubling offline earnings, activating a boost or returning an escaped prisoner. There are no forced ads.

Ads start only after you accept this Privacy Policy. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. In-app purchases

You can buy gems, coins and cosmetic skins with real money. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your card number or billing details. We receive only the product, price and transaction information needed to deliver the purchase and to restore skins after reinstalling the Game.

6. Leaderboard

When you are signed in to the Game’s online services, your income per second and player level are sent to the leaderboard together with your nickname. The leaderboard is visible to all players.

  • If you do not choose a nickname, a random name is assigned automatically.
  • Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.
  • Nicknames are limited to letters, digits and a few symbols.

7. Notifications and device features

  • Notifications (for example, that offline income is ready to collect or a daily reward is waiting) are created and scheduled locally on your device. The Game asks for permission first, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings. We do not use push notification servers.
  • Vibration is used for game feedback and can be turned off in the Game settings.
  • The Game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

8. Your consent and choices

  • When you first start the Game, we ask you to accept this Privacy Policy. Analytics and ads start only after you accept it. If we change the policy significantly, the Game will ask you again.
  • Connecting Google Play Games is optional. Without it, your progress is linked to a random player ID.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (see section 4).
  • You can turn off notifications in your device settings and vibration in the Game settings.
  • You can delete your local progress at any time in the Game: Settings → Reset.

9. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA or the UK under the GDPR, or in California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

To request deletion of your cloud save, leaderboard entry and analytics data, or to exercise any other right, e-mail us at [contact e-mail]. To help us find your data, please include your leaderboard nickname and, if you connected it, the Google Play Games account you use. We will reply within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Data retention and security

  • Local progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud saves and leaderboard entries are kept while you play and are deleted on request.
  • Analytics data is kept in pseudonymous form only as long as needed to improve the Game, in line with the retention periods of the services listed in section 3.

Data is sent to the services above over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose reputable providers and keep the data we process to a minimum.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They use safeguards required by law for such transfers, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Other versions of the Game

The version of the Game distributed outside Google Play (for example on itch.io) does not include ads, in-app purchases or Google Play Games sign-in. Sections 4, 5 and the Google Play parts of this policy do not apply to that version. All other sections apply.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. When we make significant changes, the Game will ask you to accept the new version.
